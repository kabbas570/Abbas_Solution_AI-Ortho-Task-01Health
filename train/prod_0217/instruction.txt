Goals: anterior alignment 

Upper arch 
Stage 1 - perform all movements together 
1. Compress U7s 1-2mm
2. Expand U654 RHS and LHS
3. Do not allow movement of the upper labial segment in stage 1
Stage 2 - movement only after stage 1
1. Align upper 3-3 with mild proclination and IPR

Lower arch 
Stage 1 - perform all movement together 
1. Compress L7s 1-2mm
2. Expand LR456 LL456
3. Mild alignment of the anterior teeth with IPR at straight line contacts only in stage 1
Stage 2 - movements only after stage 1
1. Once straight line access available perform further lower arch IPR to align L3-3
