Goals: anterior alignment and space closure 

Upper arch 
Stage 1 - perform all movements together 
1. Maintain buccal segments 
2. Correct crowding with proclination of the anterior teeth and increase the palatal root torque by 8 degrees this should increase the overjet to allow space closure 
1. Once inclination of the upper anterior teeth has improved close spaces maintaining palatal root torque

Lower arch 
Stage 1 - perform all movement together 
1. Maintain buccal segments
2. Correct crowding with proclination of the anterior teeth and increase the lingual root torque by 8 degrees 
1. Once inclination of the lower anterior teeth has improved close spaces maintaining lingual root torque
